Guide & Shortcuts

Interactive annotation & OCR atlas of the 15th-century Voynich Manuscript (Yale Beinecke MS 408) — 189 single pages and 38 foldout panels across 8 thematic sections.

⊞ Visual Folio Atlas (G)

Browse the entire manuscript visually by section (Herbal, Zodiac, Bathing, Cosmological, Pharmaceutical, Recipes, Foldouts) with lazy-loaded parchment thumbnails.

5 · Live Reading Transcript

Read every row of Voynich script (EVA-2 font + Latin EVA transliteration) synced with the parchment. Hover any line to highlight it on the scan; click to zoom in and compare OCR vs the ZL transliteration.

⇄ Split-Screen Compare (C)

In Ink and Drawings modes, toggle Compare to drag a vertical curtain across the folio — comparing raw parchment against extracted ink, multispectral maps, or AI vs Human masks.

✎ Contributing Edits

Fix page boundaries, holes, drawing masks, or row lines directly in the browser. Hold Space + drag to pan while editing (trackpad-friendly!). Open ✎ Edits in the top bar anytime to download or submit.

Glossary: EVA = Extensible Voynich Alphabet (standard Latin transcription) · ZL = Zandbergen-Landini reference transliteration · Hi-SAM = neural text-segmentation ink mask · Multispectral = 38-band spectral scan revealing faded ink on 10 published folios.
